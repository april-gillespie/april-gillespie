# April Gillespie

**Electrical engineer · Systems thinker · Builder**

Hello, I am April. I like understanding a difficult problem, building something useful, and figuring out what it takes to get from one to the other.

My path has taken me through aerospace systems, field applications, and technical enablement, with hands-on software projects alongside that work. I enjoy the detail of how a system works and the bigger question of what people need from it.

I often find myself working at the connections: between an idea and a plan, between people with different expertise, or between a finished task and a usable result. I like making those connections clearer and taking responsibility for seeing the work through.

## A few ways that takes shape

- **[Assist](https://april-gillespie-ai-portfolio.aprilgillespie.chatgpt.site/#work):** a personal household application connecting a local language model to a Flask backend. I designed the flow from a proposed action through validation and user confirmation. Source code is private.
- **[Intake and triage](https://github.com/april-gillespie/ai-workflow-portfolio/blob/main/docs/case-studies/enterprise-ai-intake-triage-workflow.md):** a documented prototype using fictional requests to explore scope, structured outputs, and clearer handoffs.
- **[A network investigation](https://github.com/april-gillespie/ai-workflow-portfolio/blob/main/docs/case-studies/home-network-diagnostic-ai-assisted.md):** a small example of tracing a frustrating symptom back through the system before recommending a change.

The [public project collection](https://github.com/april-gillespie/ai-workflow-portfolio) includes the reasoning, artifacts, and limitations behind these and other examples.

Technical education is another part of my experience, including contributions to the shared [ASU–Siemens PCB design certificate](https://careercatalyst.asu.edu/programs/pcb-design-fundamentals-certificate/). It brings together technical understanding, communication, and delivery.

## What matters to me

I value thoughtful decisions, clear responsibilities, and people who feel able to contribute. I enjoy sharing context, making a difficult idea easier to understand, and helping someone see how their piece fits into the whole.

I want to create things that positively affect far more people than I could reach one person at a time. That is what draws me toward larger technical challenges, complex programs, and the responsibility of helping a team grow.
